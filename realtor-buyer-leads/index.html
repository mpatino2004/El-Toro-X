FAQ

Common first-time buyer questions

How much do I need for a down payment?

Many first-time buyers put down 3–10%, not 20%. FHA loans start at 3.5% down. We'll review programs you may qualify for during your consultation.

Do I need to be pre-approved before we look at homes?

Not always, but pre-approval makes your offers stronger and helps us search in the right price range. I can recommend lenders who work well with first-time buyers.

Who pays the realtor commission?

Commission structures vary by market and transaction. As your buyer's agent, my job is to represent your interests — we'll discuss how compensation works in your area on our first call.

How long does it take to buy a home?

From accepted offer to closing, expect 30–45 days. Finding the right home can take a few weeks to a few months depending on your criteria and market conditions.

What if I lose a bidding war?

It happens in competitive markets. We'll adjust strategy — offer structure, timing, or neighborhoods — until we find the right fit. Patience and preparation win.
